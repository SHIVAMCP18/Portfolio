And that brings us back to our question. How did syrup kill twenty-one people? Not by bad luck, and not by a bomb. It was a giant tank, built by a man who was not an engineer, tested by no one, and painted brown to hide the warnings. So Massachusetts and other states began to require something that sounds obvious today. Before a big structure is built, a licensed engineer or architect must check the calculations and sign their name to them. That kind of signature is now standard across the country. Every bridge, tower, and tank you walk past today depends on that idea.
